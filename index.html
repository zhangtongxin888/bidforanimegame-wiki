Quick answers

Before you head to the table

What do you do in Bid for Anime?

You join an auction table, bid against another player, and add a character to your collection when you win. The collection then generates money for continued progression.

How do you roll rarer characters?

Upgrade Luck. The official experience description identifies Luck as the route to rolling rarer characters, without publishing exact odds.

Which variants are verified?

The official experience page names four variants: Golden, Diamond, Galaxy, and RAINBOW.
