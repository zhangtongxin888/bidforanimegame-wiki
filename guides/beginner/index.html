Part 01 · orient

A four-move quick start

Use this sequence to understand what each auction contributes before you worry about longer collection goals.

  1. 01

    Find the auction flow

    Join an auction table and identify the other player in the contest before committing to the bid.

  2. 02

    Choose an exit point

    Practical heuristic: decide what you are willing to spend before the bidding becomes competitive, and let the auction go when it passes that point.

  3. 03

    Turn wins into momentum

    A won character enters your collection, and the collection earns money that can support the next round of progress.

  4. 04

    Invest in better rolls

    Use Luck upgrades as your route toward rarer character rolls instead of expecting every auction to produce the same result.

FIELD NOTE

A practical heuristic is a decision aid, not a hidden game rule. Adjust your exit point as you learn, but do not turn it into a claim about exact value or probability.

Part 03 · stay disciplined

Your limit is a plan, not a game statistic

The official sources do not provide a universal best bid. Choose a personal exit point, observe the other player, and leave when the contest passes what you decided.
