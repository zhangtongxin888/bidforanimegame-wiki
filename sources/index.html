Verification policy

Facts and advice stay separate

  • Game facts must match an official or first-party page.
  • Advice is labeled as a practical heuristic or a suggestion to try.
  • Missing numbers remain missing; we do not fill gaps with estimates.
  • Unsupported character lists, rankings, codes, values, and formulas are not published.
